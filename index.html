01 How it works

Drop it in. Walk away.

Three steps. Runs on your machine. No editing skills, no uploads, no credits.

01

Drop a video

Any length, any common format — mp4, mov, mkv, webm. Put it in the FLIC input folder and it starts on its own.

02

FLIC does the work

It transcribes the whole thing, scores every moment for energy, emotion and hooks, then picks the strongest. All of it runs locally with a small AI model. Nothing leaves your computer.

03

Post

Up to 10 vertical clips land in your output folder: face-tracked crop, animated captions, filler words and dead air removed, a hook line written for each one. About 7 minutes for a 30-minute video.

The math

05 FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything worth knowing before you buy.

How many clips do I actually get?

Up to 10 per video. A 30-minute talking video usually lands 8 to 10. Shorter videos give fewer. FLIC only cuts moments with real speech in them, so a silent or music-only file won't produce clips.

Is it a subscription?

No. $17.99 USD once. Lifetime updates, no monthly fee, no API costs, no credits.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. FLIC runs entirely on your own computer. Your videos never get uploaded anywhere.

Do I need to show my face?

No. It works on any long video with speech. Faceless-friendly by design.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+ and 8 GB of RAM. The installer handles Python, FFmpeg, the local AI model and everything else. You run one file.

How do I get it after buying?

Checkout is through Whop. You download a ZIP, double-click one file, and the whole environment sets itself up.

Refunds?

FLIC is a digital product delivered instantly, so all sales are final. Read the requirements above before you buy, and reach out if anything in setup gives you trouble.
