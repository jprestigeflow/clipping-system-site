## Description

FLIC turns one long video into up to 10 ready-to-post vertical clips, automatically, on your own computer.

Drop a podcast, stream, VOD or talking-head upload into a folder. FLIC transcribes it, scores every moment for energy, emotion and hooks, and exports the strongest ones as 9:16 clips with animated captions, a face-tracked crop, filler words and dead air removed, and a hook line written for each clip. About 7 minutes for a 30-minute video.

It runs fully on your machine with a small local AI model. Your videos never get uploaded anywhere. No subscription, no credits, no API bills. Pay once, run as many videos as you want.

**What you get**
- Up to 10 clips per video, unlimited videos
- Vertical 9:16 export with animated word-by-word captions
- Face-tracking crop that stays on the speaker
- Filler word and dead-air removal, audio normalization

- Every export unique (no two files identical)
- Windows and macOS, one-click installer

**What you need**
